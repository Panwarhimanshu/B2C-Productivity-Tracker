Who can do what, and how to do it

The B2C Task Tracker runs on three roles — Counsellor, Head of Department, and Super Admin — each scoped to see and edit a different slice of the organisation. This guide walks through every screen a role has access to, in the order you'd actually use them.

App · B2C Daily Task Tracker Roles covered · 3 Last updated · August 2026

Overview

Every account belongs to exactly one department, and a department is named after the country it works — Canada, UK, USA, Germany, Dubai, or Europe. That mapping is what quietly scopes almost everything below.

Why this matters

A Head of Department only ever sees their own department's numbers — dashboard, reports, logs, org chart, all of it. If Anil is HOD of Canada, UK's figures simply don't appear anywhere in his account. Super Admin is the only role with an org-wide view.

Getting started

The same for every role — the top bar and the dashboard shell.

Signing in

Use the email and password your Super Admin set up for you at /login. There's no self-service signup — every account is created by a Super Admin (see User Management).

The top bar

  • Theme toggle (sun/moon icon) — switches the whole app between light and dark.
  • Notification bell — shows a red count of unread alerts. Every counsellor's report submission notifies their department's HOD and all Super Admins. Click a notification to mark it read, or Mark all read.
  • Your name, top-right — opens My Profile and Logout.

Reading the dashboard

Every role lands on Dashboard after login (Super Admin sees it labelled Organisation Dashboard). It has four parts, stacked top to bottom:

  1. Period selector — Today, Last 7 Days, Last 15 Days, or Last 30 Days. Changes the KPI cards and charts below it; it does not change target figures, which are always this calendar month.
  2. KPI cards — Total Reports, Admissions, Submitted, Modified (plus Total Users and Active Departments for Super Admin).
  3. Profile by Country — Coaching / Admission per country, each with a small badge: ✓ Target 5 means this month's target is met, ✗ 3 short of 5 means it isn't yet. No badge at all means no target has been set for that country this month.
  4. Communication & Follow-up — totals rolled up from every report in scope for the selected period.
Counsellor

Counsellor guide

Your world is your own reports, your own performance, and your own department's org chart. You can't see or touch anyone else's data.

Submitting your daily report Submit Report

One report per calendar day. The date picker only allows today — you can't back-fill a missed day or submit ahead of time.

  1. Open Submit Report. Your name, employee ID and email are filled in automatically; today's date is pre-selected.
  2. Section 1 · Profile shows one row per country your department covers (almost always just one). Fill in Coaching, Admission, Revenue and the rest of the day's figures for that country.
  3. If you enter a Coaching count of 1 or more, a small amber-bordered field appears under it for every unit — Product 1, Product 2, and so on. Each one needs the name of the product sold before you can submit. Coaching = 2 means two product names, no exceptions.
  4. Communication — log Zoom Meetings, Calls Made and Meetings as counts, paste their recording/sheet/MOM links if you have them, log WhatsApp Message Sent as a count, and set Task Module Filled to Yes, No or Pending.
  5. Section 2 · Follow-up Tasks — for each of the 9 standard tasks (see full list), enter how many you completed today and an optional remark.
  6. Section 3 · Extra Initiatives — Leads Committed and Leads Generated.
  7. Write a one-line Summary of your day, then Submit Report.
The one rule that trips people up

Coaching count and product names must match exactly, or the form — and the server — will reject the submission with a message telling you how many are still missing.

Viewing and fixing past reports My Reports

Filter by period, then View any report to see it read-only. The Edit pencil only appears on today's report — once midnight passes, that day's report is locked even to you; ask your HOD if it needs a correction.

Every edit changes the status badge from Submitted to Modified and records who changed it and when — visible to your HOD in Report Logs.

Tracking your target Performance

The Targets card shows Coaching / Admission as a progress bar per country: green at 100%+, amber from 60–99%, red below 60%. Below that, standard KPI cards and charts for the selected period.

Your department's org chart My Department

Read-only. Shows your HOD, every Counsellor in the department, and any sub-teams (e.g. Onshore, Visa Team) your HOD or Super Admin has set up, each as a card with photo, designation, phone/email, and what to contact them for.

Your profile Profile

Upload or remove your profile photo (auto-cropped square, under 5MB). Name, email, employee ID, role, department and joining date are shown for reference — editing those is a Super Admin action, done through User Management.

Head of Department

HOD guide

Everything below is scoped to your own department. You see every Counsellor under you, but nothing from any other department.

Reviewing every report in your department All Reports

  1. Filter by period and, optionally, by a specific Counsellor.
  2. View any report read-only, or Edit to correct it — unlike a Counsellor, you're not limited to today's date.
  3. Click Export to download an Excel workbook for the current filter: one sheet with a row per report per country, plus a second Target Achievement sheet — one row per Counsellor per country with a target set this month, showing target vs. achieved and an Achieved / Not Achieved status for Coaching, Admission and Revenue.

Auditing changes Report Logs

Every submission and every edit is logged with a timestamp, who did it, what changed, and their IP address — filterable by period and role. Only entries for your own department's Counsellors appear.

Maintaining your org chart My Department

Same tree view Counsellors see, but you can add, edit or remove people in it — including yourself's listing, other Counsellors' contact cards, and any sub-team you want to introduce (type a new team name, or pick an existing one from the suggestions).

What you can't do here: create a new department, rename or delete your department, or change which Counsellors/HODs are mapped into it — all three are Super Admin actions.

What's off-limits to HOD

  • Creating login accounts (only Super Admin adds users)
  • Setting monthly targets — you can see the target figures on every report and dashboard, but only Super Admin edits them
  • Seeing any department other than your own, anywhere in the app
Super Admin

Super Admin guide

Full, org-wide access — every department's reports, users, targets and org chart. All-Reports, Report Logs and the Dashboard work exactly as they do for HOD, just without the department boundary (and with an optional department filter where useful). Below are the three screens unique to this role.

Managing accounts User Management

  1. Click Add User and fill in Full Name, Email, Password, Employee ID, Joining Date, Role and Department. Editing an existing user works the same way — leave the password field blank to keep their current one.
  2. Toggle Inactive to view deactivated accounts. Deactivating a user (the person-with-a-slash icon) is a soft removal — it blocks their login without deleting their history; Reactivate reverses it.
  3. Search by name or email with the search box, top right.

Bulk import via the Import button accepts two kinds of CSV — download the sample file from the modal to get the exact column order.

ImportColumnsBehaviour
Users name, email, password, role, designation, employeeId, department, joiningDate Existing emails are updated (password kept unless a new one is given); new emails are created (password required, 6+ characters). department must match an existing department name exactly.
Targets email, country, year, month, coachingTarget, admissionTarget Matched to an existing user by email — the user must already exist.

Structuring departments Department Management

  1. Click Add Department — name it after the country it represents (e.g. Canada) so daily reports automatically scope to the right country row, give it a short description, then tick which HODs and Counsellors belong in it. Moving someone here reassigns them away from wherever they were before — the form shows "currently: X" as a warning.
  2. Click any department card to expand its full org chart — HOD, Counsellors, and every sub-team — and edit it the same way an HOD edits their own (see My Department above), for any department.
  3. Delete a department to remove it — anyone mapped to it becomes unassigned, they are not deleted as users.

Setting monthly targets Monthly Targets

Pick a Country, Month and Year, and you'll get every Counsellor in that country's department in a table. Click the pencil on a row to set Coaching, Admission and Revenue targets for that Counsellor, that country, that month — "achieved" underneath each field updates live from their daily reports as the month goes on.

Targets are per Counsellor, per country, per calendar month — there's no yearly or department-wide target, by design, so each person's number reflects only their own mapped country.

Target achievement badges

✓ Target 5 — this month's Coaching or Admission target has been met or exceeded.

✗ 3 short of 5 — still below target, with the exact gap shown.

No badge — no target has been set for that country this month; there's nothing to compare against yet.

These always reflect the current calendar month, no matter which period (Today / 7 / 15 / 30 days) is selected on the dashboard — targets are inherently monthly.

Report status

Submitted — as originally filed, untouched since.

Modified — edited at least once, by the Counsellor (same day only) or by an HOD/Super Admin (any time). Report Logs shows exactly who and when.

Printing a report

Open any report and click Print — output is formatted to a single A4 portrait page regardless of how much data it holds.
